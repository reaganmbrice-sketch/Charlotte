Basket position at the point

1.0 m below puts the point at about chest height. The stop-short distance keeps the basket off the wall.

Clearance point (optional)

Something the boom has to get past, such as the eaves of a single-storey extension. The check warns if the boom or basket passes within 0.5 m of it.

Bluetooth lasers

If the laser has a keyboard mode (many Bluetooth models do), pair it with the phone, tap the distance box and press measure. The reading types itself in and the cursor moves to the next box.

    Opening the map file

    The map file (.kml) opens in Google Earth on a phone or computer. To add it to Google Maps, go to google.com/mymaps, create a map and choose Import. Each machine position shows as a pin with its 10.4 m reach circle, and each point with a bearing shows as its own pin.
